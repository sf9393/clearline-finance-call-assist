# Unauthorized Payment & Fraud Intake

**Synthetic internal policy · FRAUD-UP · Version 3.0 · June 10, 2026**

## Agents may say

- The report can be documented and sent for specialist review.
- The review process can be explained at a general level.

## Agents may not say

- “This is fraud,” “you are not liable,” or “the payment will be reversed.”
- Any promise about an investigation result.

## Required verification and escalation

1. Complete identity verification before discussing transaction details.
2. Capture the report through the unauthorized-payment intake workflow.
3. **Urgently route** to the Fraud & Disputes queue during the call.

## Approved language

“I’m sorry this payment is concerning. Once I verify your information, I’ll document the report and connect it to the review process.”
