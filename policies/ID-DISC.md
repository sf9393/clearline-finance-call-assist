# Identity Verification & Disclosure Controls

**Synthetic internal policy · ID-DISC · Version 4.2 · June 25, 2026**

## Agents may say

- General process information before verification.
- That verification is required before account details can be discussed.

## Agents may not say

- Any account balance, payment status, transaction detail, payoff amount, or available option before verification.
- Confirmation that an account or transaction exists before successful verification.

## Required verification and escalation

1. Use the approved verification sequence before account-specific discussion.
2. If verification fails, stop account-specific discussion and follow the verification-failure workflow.
3. Give only general process information until verification succeeds.

## Approved language

“Before I access or discuss account details, I’ll need to verify your identity. I can share general information about the process while we complete that step.”
