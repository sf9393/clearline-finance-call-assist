# Financial Hardship Assistance

**Synthetic internal policy · FIN-HRD · Version 2.1 · May 1, 2026**

## Agents may say

- A brief hardship summary can be documented for review.
- A specialist team may review support options.

## Agents may not say

- “You qualify,” “I can change your arrangement,” or financial advice.
- Any promise about the review outcome.

## Required verification and escalation

1. Complete identity verification before account discussion.
2. Determine whether the difficulty is short-term or ongoing; record only the minimum approved summary.
3. Route the case to the hardship review queue. Do not negotiate nonstandard arrangements.

## Approved language

“I’m sorry this is difficult. I can document a brief summary and have the appropriate team review the support options that may be available.”
