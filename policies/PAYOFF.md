# Payoff Quote Requests

**Synthetic internal policy · PAYOFF · Version 1.2 · March 20, 2026**

## Agents may say

- An official payoff quote can be requested after verification.
- The quote has a validity date and required disclosures.

## Agents may not say

- An estimated payoff balance or an amount recalled from memory.
- That a quote remains valid after its stated validity date.

## Required verification and escalation

1. Complete identity verification before account discussion.
2. Generate the quote only through the approved payoff-quote tool.
3. Read the validity date and required disclosures exactly as displayed.

## Approved language

“After I verify your information, I can request an official payoff quote and review its validity date with you.”
