# Payment Extension & Due-Date Support

**Synthetic internal policy · PAY-EXT · Version 1.4 · April 15, 2026**

## Agents may say

- Extension options may be reviewed after successful verification.
- Eligibility is subject to the approved account-review workflow.

## Agents may not say

- “Your extension is approved.”
- “Your due date will be moved,” or “fees will be waived.”

## Required verification and escalation

1. Complete identity verification before account-specific discussion.
2. Acknowledge the concern and use the approved eligibility workflow.
3. Escalate to Financial Hardship Assistance if inability to pay is ongoing or tied to material financial disruption.

## Approved language

“I’m sorry you’re dealing with that. After I verify your information, I can review the payment-support options available for your account.”
