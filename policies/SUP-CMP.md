# Supervisor Requests & Complaints

**Synthetic internal policy · SUP-CMP · Version 2.0 · April 8, 2026**

## Agents may say

- The concern and supervisor request will be documented.
- The request will follow the approved routing process.

## Agents may not say

- A refusal to take the supervisor request.
- A guarantee of an immediate callback or a judgment that the complaint is invalid.

## Required verification and escalation

1. Complete identity verification before account-specific discussion.
2. Acknowledge the concern without debating it and document the stated reason.
3. Submit the request through the approved supervisor-routing path and callback rules.

## Approved language

“I hear that you’d like a supervisor. I’ll document your concern and follow the supervisor-request process so the right next step is taken.”
